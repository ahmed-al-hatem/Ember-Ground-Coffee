The roastery

Small-batch, by conviction

We started Ember & Ground in 2018 in a railway arch in Hackney Wick. The premise was simple: roast less, roast better. Source directly where we can, work with importers we trust where we can't. Keep the batches small enough that we notice when something changes — and keep changing it until it's right.

We roast on a 15-kilo drum roaster, which means every batch takes our full attention. There's no coasting on automation here. The result is coffee with a clear point of view — not safe, not generic, not designed by committee.

Read the full story

Direct relationships

We visit every farm we work with. We pay above Fair Trade minimums because the price of coffee doesn't reflect the labour that grows it.

Transparent roasting

Every bag ships with a roast date and full origin notes. We don't hide roast profiles behind marketing language — the data is yours.

Zero compromise on green

We reject lots that don't meet our cup score threshold. We'd rather have gaps in our range than fill them with coffee we can't stand behind.
